## Scope and mode

Redesign the existing Personal Bests route as an Operate surface. Keep the current route, data, filters, view-trip actions, and per-species progression, but replace the cramped card wall with a more legible record-led composition.

## Audience, job, action/task, proof/content, and constraints

The trusted logbook operator is reviewing the largest recorded catches after a trip or across a season. They need to scan the current leaders, change the year/month/ranking scope, compare species records, and open the source trip. Proof is the existing catch data, photos, measurements, dates, locations, lures, and progression history. Preserve the existing CSS color roles, light/dark themes, semantic labels, IDs, responsive behavior, and plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript stack; do not invent claims or require new assets.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

Use a catch-ledger composition: the page opens with a strong title and compact filter rail, follows with a connected summary strip, then gives the leading catch a featured evidence panel while the remaining species records read as compact entries. Progression becomes a clearly separated history section with consistent timeline rhythm. The memorable moment is seeing the current leader as a real, traceable catch rather than a generic metric tile.

## Unresolved decisions

No product decisions are unresolved. The visual treatment should remain restrained enough for repeated use, with green reserved for active/positive emphasis and blue retained as a secondary information color.
